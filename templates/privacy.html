Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password. When you use the app, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in a secured database accessible only to you. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no system is completely immune to risk — please use a strong, unique password.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your financial information is private and will only be disclosed if required by law or to prevent fraud.

5. Third-Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts. This may result in your browser making a request to Google's servers. No personal or financial data is shared with Google. No other third-party services are used.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated expense records and personal information will be permanently removed from our systems.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also request an export of your data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact information on our website.
